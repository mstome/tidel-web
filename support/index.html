Devices

What do I need?

A phone with GPS. That is the whole list. iPhone on iOS 15 or later, or Android 7.0 or later. Everything else — a watch, an external receiver — is optional.

Does my watch work?

Apple Watch: yes, on iPhone. Record the ride as a workout, and Tidel reads the route and heart rate from Apple Health.

Garmin: a small Tidel app on the watch sends the session to your Tidel account; Garmin itself gets nothing from Tidel.

Polar: connect your Polar account in the app, and Tidel fetches the sessions from Polar.

Suunto, Coros and others: if the watch writes workouts to Apple Health or Health Connect, Tidel imports them like any other workout; otherwise export the ride as a GPX file and open it in Tidel.

Tidel is not affiliated with any of these companies.

Is there a difference between iPhone and Android?

The analysis is identical. The difference is where workouts come from: Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android, and GPX files on both.

Recording and importing

Do I need signal at the track?

No. Recording, importing and analysis all happen on the phone. Most tracks have no coverage anyway. Your sessions sync to your account when you are back on a network.

How do I import a ride I already recorded?

Go to More → Import. From there you can:

  • pull workouts straight from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android),
  • open a GPX file from any app or watch,
  • drop in an Apple Health export zip if you want your whole history at once,
  • download sessions from a paired RaceBox.

Tidel finds the track, sets the finish line and splits the laps on its own.

It imported my bike ride to the shops as a session

Tidel filters out rides that do not look like laps, but it is not perfect. Delete the session from the session list; it is marked deleted and disappears for you and your friends. From Tidel 1.21 the app deletes its raw GPS file; one it does not delete is unreadable, goes into the deletion queue when the session is deleted, and is removed by the nightly job.

Lap times and GPS

Why did I get fewer laps than I rode?

A lap only counts if the GPS actually shows you crossing the finish line, in the right direction, with no gap in the recording. The usual causes are:

  • the finish line sits somewhere you do not really pass, or passes it at an angle,
  • the GPS dropped out for a few seconds,
  • you stopped for long enough that the lap is not a lap any more.

Open Track setup from the map and move the finish line to a spot you cross cleanly every lap, ideally on a straight. Tidel re-counts immediately, and tells you why each discarded pass was discarded.

How accurate are the times?

Accurate enough to compare your own laps with each other, which is the point. A phone gives roughly one position per second; a RaceBox gives twenty-five. Neither is transponder timing, and neither should be used to settle a result or claim a record.

What is the “best sectors combined” time?

Your fastest time in each sector, added together. It is a calculation, not a lap you rode — it shows what the day was worth if you put your best parts on the same lap. Tidel drops any sector where the GPS had a gap or you stopped, and marks the number when it had to.

RaceBox

Which boxes work?

RaceBox Mini, Mini S and Micro, over Bluetooth. Tidel reads 25 Hz position plus altitude, satellite count, heading and three-axis G-force. Tidel has no partnership with RaceBox — it is simply a receiver the app can talk to.

It will not connect

Only one app can talk to a RaceBox at a time. Close the RaceBox app completely, including from the app switcher, and disconnect any other phone that has been paired to it. The box keeps recording on its own even when nothing is connected, so you will not lose the session.

Then open More → My devices and connect again.

Your account

I never made an account. Do I have one?

Yes: in the beta an anonymous one is created automatically, with no email and no password, so your sessions follow you to a new phone. When Tidel opens to everyone, the account needs an email address and a password. You can see its 8-character share code under More → My share code.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup from More → Backup on the old phone and open it on the new one; it carries sessions, tracks, settings and trophies. Signed in to the same account, the sessions and synced settings also come back from the server.

How do I delete everything?

In the app: More → Your account → Delete my account, or without the app tidel.no/delete-account. There is a 7-day grace period, and the night after it a nightly job completes the deletion (you can also finish it with “Complete deletion now” in the app); the page says what is deleted and what is not.

Is there a web version?

A web version is being built for deeper analysis on a big screen; it is in private testing. You attach an email address to the account in the app: More → Your account → Link an email to the account.

Price and privacy

What does it cost?

Lap times are free, and stay free. No ads. When Tidel opens to everyone, you create an account so your seasons follow you between phones. Paid extras may come later; we say so in advance, and the rides you have recorded stay yours.

Who can see my rides?

Your sessions are private by default: the ride, the GPS trace and the heart rate are yours, and nobody sees them unless you deliberately share a session.

Three things are shared without you asking: your best lap time on a track goes on that track’s own list, with the rider name you show, by default; a new track you ride goes to the shared catalogue with your name as a credit; and your riding on the track itself goes, without name or account, into the shared track model. Turn the list off for one session while reviewing it, or for good under More → Privacy, where you can also opt out of the track model; the full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Still stuck?

Write to post@tidel.no. Tell us the phone you use, what you did, and what happened instead. If it is about one particular session, the date and track are enough to find it.
