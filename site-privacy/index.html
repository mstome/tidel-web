Website privacy

This page is about the website you are reading right now. It is separate from the app’s privacy policy, which covers the Tidel app for iOS and Android.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · App privacy policy: English · norsk

No cookies. No tracking across websites. No advertising, and nothing sold to anyone. tidel.no does not ask for cookie consent because it does not store or read anything on your device. The one thing it asks you to agree to is the waiting list on the front page — and only if you fill it in yourself.

What is measured

The front page, /download/, the shared-lap page /r/, the buddy link /v/ and the track pages under /bane/ count visits so the developer can tell whether anyone is finding them. The measurement is done by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is designed to work without cookies and without building a profile of you. On /r/ and /v/ the lap or the buddy code travels after # in the address; it never leaves your browser, and the visit is counted on the plain path only.

The address of the page you openedTo see which pages people actually read
The referring site, if you came from a linkTo see where visitors come from
Browser, operating system and rough screen sizeTo know which devices the site must work on

What is not measured

  • No cookies are set, and nothing is written to local storage.
  • No fingerprinting. You are not given an identifier that follows you between visits.
  • Your IP address is not stored. It is used momentarily to derive the country, then discarded.
  • Nothing is shared with advertising networks, and no data is sold.
  • The measurement cannot be linked to a Tidel account, a session, a lap time or a position.

The waiting list

The front page has one field: tell me when Tidel opens. Nothing is stored unless you type your address, tick the box yourself and press the button. The box is never pre-ticked. The form is for adults; under-15s cannot consent to marketing email themselves.

What is storedWhy
Your email addressTo send you the message you asked for
When you signed up, and the exact wording you ticked (as a version number)To be able to show that, and how, you consented
Your language (Norwegian or English)To write to you in it
When you confirmed, and when you unsubscribedSo mail goes only to confirmed addresses, and never again after you leave
When the opening email was sent to you, and how many times it could not be sentSo you get it once, and never twice
  • Double confirmation. You first get one email with a link. You are not on the list until you open it and press the button on the page it opens, and an unconfirmed address is deleted after 30 days.
  • What you get: one email when Tidel opens to everyone, and at most one a month until then. No more than that.
  • Unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of every email, including the first one. The address is then marked as unsubscribed so it is not emailed again, and removed after 90 days.
  • The address is used for this alone. It is not linked to a Tidel account, and never shared or sold. The form sends your address, the consent version and your language — nothing else.
  • Flood guard. To stop one sender from filling the list, the database counts sign-ups per day against a short code of your email address and a salted hash of the network address the request came from. Neither is stored next to your email, and both are deleted after two days.

The legal bit

For the visit counting, the lawful basis is legitimate interest under GDPR article 6(1)(f): knowing whether a website is read at all is a normal part of running one. Because no information is stored on or read from your device, the Norwegian ekomlov § 3-15 (the «cookie rule») does not require consent here.

For the waiting list, the lawful basis is your consent, GDPR article 6(1)(a), given the way the Norwegian Marketing Control Act § 15 requires: by an active tick, documented, and withdrawable at any time with the unsubscribe link.

Processors: Cloudflare for the counting (developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics); for the waiting list, Supabase stores the list in the European Union (Ireland), the same database as the app, and Resend sends the emails from no-reply@tidel.no. Both act on Tidel’s instructions only.

Turning it off

Any content blocker that blocks static.cloudflareinsights.com stops the measurement completely, and the site works exactly the same without it. Sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control from your browser is also respected — the script is not loaded at all in that case. The waiting list needs nothing turned off: it does nothing until you fill it in.
