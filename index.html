02Find your time

You have already
ridden a faster lap.
Just not all at once.

Tidel takes your best time in each sector and stitches them into one lap. That gap is time you have already put on the board.

A stitched lap is a calculation, not a lap you rode. Tidel says so on the number, and drops any sector where the GPS dropped out or you stopped.

04A season, not a session

Every session lands on the same axis, per track. Lap times are never compared across tracks — a best lap here and a best lap there are not the same number.

−44.58 S

Four sessions, one track, one finish line — every lap on this graph is measured the same way. 22 July was a bad day, and Tidel leaves it in.
