Tidel is in beta on both platforms. Pick yours below — the status is exactly what it says.

What you need

A phone with GPS. That is the whole list. A watch, a RaceBox or a GPX file will all work too, but none of them are required to get a lap time.

No account needed

Tidel works without signing up. There is no email to enter and no password to pick before your first session.

What beta means

Features change, and some things break. You get updates often, and the fastest way to get something fixed is to write and say what happened. Keep a backup from More → Backup if a season matters to you.

What it costs

Nothing. No ads, no in-app purchases. If paid features arrive later, we will say so in advance and your existing rides stay accessible.
