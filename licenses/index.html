Licenses and data sources

Tidel uses open data and open-source code from others. Here is who, what it is used for, and what the licenses require.

Last updated 15 October 2026 · applies to the Tidel app for iOS and Android, and to tidel.no · Norsk versjon: lisenser

In short. Track positions come from OpenStreetMap, satellite imagery from Esri, the ground in the 3D model from Kartverket, and the 3D view, the QR codes, the app packaging and the typeface are open source. The same is listed in the app under More → Help → Licenses and data sources, where the license texts can be read too.

1. Maps and tracks

The track register

The positions and names of most tracks in the register come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License (ODbL). Club, opening hours and contact details are collected from the tracks', clubs' and federations' own websites; the source is shown on each track page in the app.

Satellite imagery

The map tiles in the app and the images on tidel.no come from Esri World Imagery: Source: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. The tiles are fetched from the provider when the map is shown. About the service.

Driving route

The driving distance to a track in Explore is computed by openrouteservice (HeiGIT), with map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Terrain and 3D

The ground in the 3D model of the track and the relief images are built from elevation data © Kartverket, CC BY 4.0 (hoydedata.no). Outside Norway, Terrain Tiles (Tilezen/Mapzen, AWS Open Data) are used; the sources behind each model are credited in the 3D view.
