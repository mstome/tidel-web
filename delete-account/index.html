Delete your
Tidel account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Tidel app (MX Performance Tracker, developed by Magnus Linnerud Støme) and everything stored with it.

Last updated 16 November 2026

Option 1 — in the app

If you still have Tidel installed, this is the fastest way and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Tidel
  2. Go to More (bottom right)
  3. Scroll to Your account
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm

Option 2 — by email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or you cannot reach the button, send us a request and we will delete the account for you.

Include your account code if you still have it — it is the 8-character code shown in the app under More → My share code. Without it, tell us anything that helps us find the right account, such as the rider name you used and roughly when you last rode.

Request deletion by email

The address is post@tidel.no. A person reads it. You get a written confirmation when the deletion is done.

What is deleted, and what is not

The list is made from the same data map as the Privacy Policy, one entry per item Tidel collects; the policy says more about each:

  • Deleted with the account: Account id and share code, Email and password, Rider name and class, Profile picture, The session, The GPS track, Lap times, Best lap on the track’s list, Heart rate, Age, weight and resting heart rate, Settings that sync, Ground profiles, Friends and buddy links, What friends see, Reactions and track days, Sensor and serial number, “Where did you hear about Tidel?”, Consent to news by email, The “Later” counter, The email log, Position while recording, Garmin connection, Polar connection, Apple Health and Health Connect, The analysis access, Deletion devices.
  • Partly (the section that covers the row says what stays): Tracks you add, Finish lines, layouts and track info, Sessions you share with friends, Training sessions, Installation id, Usage statistics, Error reports from the app, “What stopped you?”, Feedback, Tidel Pro, IP address.
  • Not deleted with the account (kept at Tidel; the section that covers the row says more): The track model, The deletion receipt, The daily ops email to the owner, Daily caps against abuse.
  • Not held by Tidel (nothing there to delete): Shared cards and links, The backup, Maps and aerial imagery, The camera, The picture you set on a friend, Map tiles, Sector passes.
  • Not linked to the account: The waiting list on tidel.no, Driving route in Explore, Visit counting on tidel.no, The version check.

Tracks, finish lines and track info you added stay in the shared catalogue when you delete your account, with your name and account id removed where they are stored with a key; the installation id on the track and the pseudonym on line proposals remain. If one rider leaving deleted the track, it would break for everyone else who rides there. A finished track model is an average with no link to you and stays (Privacy Policy, section 7).

Usage statistics, error reports and the answer to “What stopped you?” carry no account id, only the installation id; when the account is deleted, they are deleted for the phones we know are yours: the phones that uploaded your sessions and tracks or sent feedback with the account, and from Tidel 1.21 the phone you delete from; if another account has also used the phone, the statistics from it stay. Feedback is deleted with the account when it was sent with it (from Tidel 1.21), and otherwise when it came from one of the same phones; from a phone another account has also used, it is kept with the contact address you wrote. Ground profiles under jumps are deleted with the account, and a deleted account never contributes to the track model. When the account is deleted, a receipt with a salted pseudonym of the account id and counts is written; it has no id and remains. A copy of a session you shared is removed from your friend’s phone the next time their app syncs; a file they exported from it stays with them.

How long it takes

  • Immediately: the account is locked: no new sessions or track files can be uploaded to it.
  • 7 days: a grace period in which you can undo the deletion, in the app or by replying to our email.
  • After 7 days: a nightly job completes the deletion the following night; you can also complete it with “Complete deletion now” in the app, or let us finish it if you asked by email.
  • If you uninstall the app, the deletion is still completed after the grace period.
  • If you ask by email, allow up to 30 days in total; in practice it is done within a couple of days plus the grace period.

Deleting only some of it

A single session can be deleted inside the app, from the session list; it is marked deleted with its laps and record, and disappears for you and your friends. From Tidel 1.21 the app deletes its raw GPS file; one it does not delete is unreadable to anyone, goes into the deletion queue when the session is deleted, and is removed by the nightly job. You can export everything first under More → Backup; that file stays on your phone and is never touched by the server.

Questions

Anything unclear, write to post@tidel.no. See also the Privacy Policy for what is stored in the first place.
